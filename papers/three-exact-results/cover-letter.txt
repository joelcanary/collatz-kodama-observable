To:      The Editors, Experimental Mathematics
Subject: Submission — "Three Exact Results Arising from Collatz-Type Dynamics"

Dear Editors,

I submit for your consideration the enclosed paper, "Three Exact Results Arising from
Collatz-Type Dynamics: Valuation Equidistribution, Fermat Bases, and a Transcendental
Density Constant" (7 pages).

Why Experimental Mathematics. All three results began as computational observations while
studying the accelerated Collatz map, and were then settled — proved outright, reduced to
a known criterion, or formalised. The paper is written in the spirit your journal asks
for: the role of the computer is stated plainly, every numerical claim is reproducible
from a single enclosed script (verify.py), and two of the three results are additionally
machine-checked in Lean 4 (the kernel accepts them with no `sorry` and only the ambient
axioms). I regard machine-checking as the appropriate standard of evidence when the source
of a conjecture is itself a machine.

What the paper does and does not claim. It does NOT address the Collatz conjecture, and
says so repeatedly. Each of the three results stands whether or not the conjecture is true:

  (i)   an exact 2-adic law for the step valuations: for every odd base a,
        v2(a*n+1) >= k  iff  n = -a^{-1} (mod 2^k). Hence the valuation is Geom(1/2) with
        mean 2 and variance 2, independently of a, and the two valuations of the two-sided
        step 3n+-1 are anti-correlated with covariance exactly -1. (Lean-certified.)
  (ii)  the maps x -> a*x+1 admit closed-form polynomial orbits iff a-1 is a power of 2,
        i.e. a = 2^e+1. Both directions in the kernel. (Lean-certified.)
  (iii) the density constant C = 0.28627450232410874... has a Sturmian binary expansion
        and is therefore transcendental, via Ferenczi-Mauduit / Adamczewski-Bugeaud.

On novelty, honestly. These are elementary results. Their value is that they are exact
(not asymptotic or numerical), unified under one mechanism (2 is a unit), and
machine-certified — and that (iii) exhibits a concrete, previously unnamed transcendental
constant with a clean Sturmian structure. The paper also records, with explicit
counterexamples, a limitative fact that is easy to reach experimentally and get wrong: the
step valuation is a function of no fixed residue of x, so no finite automaton on residues
models the dynamics. I include this because the error recurs in the informal literature
and I think stating it precisely has value.

Disclosure. The work was AI-assisted. Every statement was verified by exact computation or
by Lean's kernel before inclusion, and every candidate that failed verification was
discarded; I mention this plainly rather than leave it implicit.

Data and code. The enclosed verify.py regenerates every number in the paper (it prints
[OK]/[FAIL] per claim and runs in under a minute). The Lean development is available and
builds with `lake build`; `#print axioms` is documented in the paper.
